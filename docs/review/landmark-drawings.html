Landmarks from the plane: all eight

Big landmarks appear in the side window on the correct side of the plane; small or far ones on the seat-back camera. On a cloudy day (real weather) the side view switches to the camera. In the room the clouds drift, the snow plume and banner cloud move, the mist rises and the camera pans.
