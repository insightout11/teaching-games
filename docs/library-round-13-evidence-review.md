# Round 13 debate evidence review

All 120 entries were read against their own motion and marked side. **54 entries were replaced**; 66 retained. Each replacement is a source-backed paraphrase in `src/data/debate-motions.json`. Source names and primary links are in [the evidence source registry](library-round-12-evidence-sources.md). The facts provide usable reasons for classroom discussion; they do not establish that a policy works everywhere. Associations are described as associations.

The unchanged entries were retained only where the fact gives a student a quotable reason for that side. Examples: OECD's homework association for a lighter workload; WHO activity guidance for trying sport; WHO sugar guidance for reducing sugary drinks; EPA's waste hierarchy for reuse and prevention; Smithsonian's breeding results for conservation; UNESCO's technology access gap against digital-only textbooks; and UNESCO's language guidance for home-language teaching. Sources should be used with their stated scope, especially where a fact is observational or concerns one country or zoo.
