# Preset Copy Report
*3–4 sentence descriptions for each Flight Plan preset. For use as UI copy, marketing, and onboarding material.*

---

## Game Day
Turn your classroom into a game show. Students compete across four back-to-back rounds — pattern recognition, grid challenges, social deduction, and a high-stakes quiz finale — with a live scoreboard tracking every point. No lesson plan required: just pick a topic, hit start, and let the competition run. Great for end-of-week energy, review sessions, or any day the class needs a win.

---

## Debate Ready
Give students the tools to argue well, not just argue loudly. The lesson opens with Mission Selector — the class votes on a topic and declares their opening stance — before diving into Fact Detective to build the evidence they'll need. Defend the Indefensible then teaches the mechanics of argumentation in a low-stakes, often hilarious warm-up round, before Hot Take Arena puts it all on the line. Opinion Shift closes the lesson by asking the only question that matters: did anyone change their mind?

---

## Vocab Blitz
The fastest way to move vocabulary from a word list into long-term memory. Students start by mapping what they already know with Vocab Radar, then sharpen recognition through Synonym Showdown and Vocab Sprint — all drawing from the same generated word set. Password closes the lesson with the real test: can students use those words naturally in conversation without ever saying them directly? Every activity in the sequence works with the same vocabulary, so words get reinforced multiple times before the lesson ends.

---

## Speaking Circle
Built for students who need more time in the room to find their voice. Character Cards gives everyone a speaking anchor at the start, Would You Rather gets opinions flowing with low-pressure binary choices, and Scene Igniter moves into scripted role-play before Conversation Rounds removes the scaffold entirely. The sequence is designed so students arrive at free conversation already warmed up and confident rather than cold.

---

## Grammar Clinic
A focused 60-minute accuracy workout for classes where grammar errors are getting in the way. Error Hunter sharpens the eye for mistakes, Sentence Scramble puts grammar rules into practice by rebuilding broken sentences, and Grammar Boss finishes with spoken production under pressure. The arc moves deliberately from spotting errors to constructing sentences to performing them — the same grammar point touched three times in three different ways.

---

## Think Tank
For classes that are ready to go deeper. Wonder Board opens the lesson with student-generated questions about the topic, turning curiosity into the engine of the whole lesson before a single answer has been given. Fact Detective then challenges students to evaluate evidence, Connections asks them to find the hidden patterns, and 20 Questions closes with a deduction game where the class must reason their way to an answer together. Built for critical thinkers, sceptics, and anyone who likes to ask why.

---

## Travel English
Real English for real travel situations. Vocab Radar introduces the language students will actually need — at the airport, the hotel, the restaurant — before Dialogue Detective puts that language in context through authentic conversation analysis. Scene Igniter adds a scripted practice round where students rehearse the situation before Conversation Rounds drops the script and lets them navigate it on their own. By the end of the lesson, students have heard, studied, practiced, and used the same functional vocabulary in four different ways.

---

## Job English
Professional communication skills for students preparing to work in English. Vocab Radar pre-loads the vocabulary of the workplace before Dialogue Detective shows how professionals actually talk on the job. Scene Igniter bridges the gap with scripted role-play, then Conversation Rounds hands it back to students to handle the situation themselves. Choose from job roles — hospitality, retail, healthcare, admin — and the whole lesson adapts to that context automatically.

---

## Creative Sprint
For classes where imagination needs a runway. Character Cards assigns a creative identity at the start, then Wonder Board gets students generating questions — what if, how, why — that become the seeds for the lesson. Scene Igniter builds those ideas into a scenario students can inhabit, and Story Sprint is where the story actually gets made. The sequence is designed so students arrive at creative production with something real to say, not a blank page.

---

## First Day
A first lesson that actually breaks the ice. Two Truths and a Lie gets students sharing something real about themselves immediately, Would You Rather builds energy through opinion and debate, and Imposter turns the group into detectives — figuring out who's hiding the secret word keeps everyone engaged and talking. Choose a social topic for a pure getting-to-know-you session, or pick a curriculum theme to warm students up to the unit ahead at the same time.
